Study tips

Small daily actions create big exam outcomes

  • Revise daily using short topic summaries and flashcards.
  • Follow a fixed weekly schedule with one major subject and two practice blocks.
  • Review previous mistakes to improve accuracy and confidence.
  • Use mock tests to build exam temperament and time awareness.
